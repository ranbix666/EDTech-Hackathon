Welcome to Prober.ai — Defend your draft with AI-supported review.

Most AI tools are designed to do the thinking for you. Prober.ai is different. This is an AI-supported writing tool that refuses to rewrite your essay. Instead, it acts as a rigorous peer reviewer, examining your writing and helping you build a strong argument.

By engaging in this knowledge co-construction, you will build stronger warrants, clarify your thesis, and master the art of argumentative writing.

Are you ready to test your text? Choose your reviewer below to begin the defense.

1
